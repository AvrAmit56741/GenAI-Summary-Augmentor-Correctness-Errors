# Verification of generator freshness in English summarization factual-consistency metric and benchmark papers

Verified 2026-11-18 against primary sources. The first-pass file checked is
`paper/research/model_freshness_en_summarization.md`.

Method: arXiv PDFs and ACL Anthology PDFs were downloaded and their text searched. Quotes below are copied
from the extracted text. PDF extraction drops hyphens, so a few compound words, such as "preTransformer", are
spelled as they came out. Venues come from the arXiv `comments` and `journal_ref` fields, Semantic Scholar's ACL
Anthology IDs, and ACL Anthology pages. "Gen" means the models that wrote the human-labelled summaries. "Judge"
means a model used as the metric. Model release years are general knowledge and were not re-checked in this pass.

## Q1. Rows marked (mem) or (unverified): venue, year and generators

### Takeaway
Most venues the first pass gave from memory are correct. There are three real corrections:
- Ramprasad et al. 2024 is **ACL 2024 main (long)**, not Findings.

## Q2. Headline claims

### Takeaway
- Tang 2023's "~10% balanced accuracy drop OLD→FtSota on CNN/DM" and "more mixed on XSum" are verbatim.
- FaithBench's detector numbers differ by version:
  - arXiv v1: 44.9–57.7% balanced accuracy (BA).
  - NAACL camera-ready: 48.7–62.3% BA, with more detectors added.
  - FaithBench samples were deliberately chosen where detectors disagreed, so near-chance BA is partly a result of how the data was selected.
- The UniSumEval, TofuEval and Ramprasad quotes are verbatim.
- FineSurE's human labels are on FRANK and REALSumm (fine-tuned models up to 2020). Its 8-LLM summaries have no human faithfulness labels.
- VeriGray's GPT-5 claims are confirmed, and there a detector reaches 83.6% BA.

### Cited Findings
- **Tang et al. 2023, verbatim** ([arXiv 2205.12854](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.12854), ACL 2023):
  - "There is a significant performance drop when evaluating the CNN/DM summaries generated by models from EXFORMER or FTSOTA instead. Approximately a 10% balanced accuracy decrease on average occurs from OLD to FTSOTA."
  - "We observe more mixed results for AGGREFACT-XSUM. Here, the trained and ChatGPT-based metrics perform best on FTSOTA and EXFORMER respectively. In fact, the ChatGPT-ZS and ChatGPT-Star metrics report new state-of-the-art results for the EXFORMER category."
  - Also verbatim:
    - "much of the recent improvement in the factuality detection space has been on summaries from older (pre-Transformer) models instead of more relevant recent summarization models"
    - "metrics which claim improved performance on SUMMAC do not show similar gains when evaluated on FTSOTA summaries"
    - Summaries in OLD "contain obvious errors, such as repetition, that can be more easily detected".
  - Caveat: "10%" is the paper's wording for a drop in BA points averaged over metrics. It is not a relative drop.
- **FaithBench, which LLMs.** CONFIRMED. There are 10 LLMs from 8 families, 66 passages × 10 LLMs = 660 samples ([arXiv 2410.13210](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.13210)):
  - GPT-4o and GPT-3.5-Turbo
  - Llama-3.1-70B and Llama-3.1-8B
  - Gemini-1.5-Flash
  - Claude-3.5-Sonnet
  - Qwen2.5-7B
  - Phi-3-mini-4k
  - Command-R
  - Mistral-7B
- **How FaithBench chose its models:** "For each family, we then selected the smallest version in its latest generation. The exceptions are the GPT and Llama series from which we select two each."

  - "human annotators identify the highest frequency of errors for summaries generated by the proprietary LLMs, followed by those generated by the open-source LLMs, and the least for those generated by the non-LLMs. This trend highlights that automated faithfulness evaluation is more challenging for summaries generated by recent LLMs compared to those generated by non-LLMs."
  - Also: "contrary to prior findings that NLI-based evaluators perform well (Tang et al., 2024b), they show much lower agreement compared to G-Eval ... Hence, non-LLM evaluators tend to perform poorly at verifying LLM-generated hallucinations."

- **TofuEval, verbatim:** "As hallucinations from more recent models are harder to detect (Tang et al., 2023a), we re-evaluate non-LLM-based and LLM-based factuality metrics using LLM-generated summaries" ([arXiv 2402.13249](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.13249)).
- **Ramprasad et al. 2024, verbatim:**
  - LLMs "generate plausible inferences, supported by circumstantial evidence in the conversation, that lack direct evidence, a pattern that is less prevalent in older models."

  - Source: [ACL 2024 long 677](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.677).
- **FineSurE, human labels.** CONFIRMED on FRANK ("2,246 summaries ... nine summarization systems on CNNDM and XSUM") and REALSumm ("2,500 summaries from 25 summarization systems ... based on CNNDM") ([arXiv 2407.00908](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.00908)).
  - Section 4.3 says "we gather summaries generated by four open-source and four proprietary LLMs ... The summaries are generated for 100 examples sourced from CNNDM, all of which are also included in REALSumm."
  - Proprietary summarizers shown: Gemini-1-pro, GPT-3.5-turbo, GPT-4-turbo and GPT-4-omni.
  - These are scored only by FineSurE. Human keyfacts exist for completeness, but there are no human faithfulness labels.
  - FRANK's generators are from 2017–2020. REALSumm's 25-system list was not re-opened, and its upper year bound of about 2020 is UNCONFIRMED here.
- **MiniCheck, LLM-AggreFact summarization subsets.** CONFIRMED as AggreFact SOTA subset (CNN, XSum) and TofuEval (MediaSum, MeetingBank). The paper reports "we do not otherwise report results on the smaller datasets within the AGGREFACT SOTA subset" ([arXiv 2404.10774](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.10774)).
  - Generators are BART, PEGASUS and T5 (FtSota) and the five TofuEval LLMs. No GPT-4 summaries are included.
- **MSumBench.** The model list is CONFIRMED (see Q1). The self-bias quote was not re-searched in this pass; the first pass marked it (v).
- **VeriGray.** CONFIRMED that the authors "employed more recent LLMs, including GPT-5 (OpenAI, 2025), DeepSeek V3-0324 ..., and Qwen3-8B" on top of FaithBench's documents and summaries ([arXiv 2510.21118 v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.21118)).
  - "Claude-3.5-Sonnet generates the fewest Unfaithful sentences (3%), surpassing the more recent GPT-5 (6%), whereas Qwen3-8B generates the most (32%)."
  - Best detector: "GPT-5 + RAG, which achieve balanced accuracy of 83.6%, hallucination F1 of 73.5%".
  - Non-LLM and hidden-state detectors score about 47–59% BA (Table 4).
  - The stale-benchmark critique of Cao & Wang "rel[ying] on old summarization models such as BART ... and PEGASUS" is verbatim.

### Inferences
- FaithBench's near-50% result should not be read as "detectors fail on modern LLM summaries" in general. The samples were filtered to cases where detectors disagreed. On VeriGray, built on largely the same documents without that filtering, frontier LLM judges reach about 84% BA, and FaithJudge reaches 84% on FaithBench itself.
- Classic NLI-style detectors (AlignScore, TrueTeacher, MiniCheck, HHEM) stay around 50–59% BA on both FaithBench and VeriGray. This supports the claim that fixed non-LLM metrics transfer poorly to modern-LLM summaries.

### Gaps
- I did not find a per-generator detector breakdown in FaithBench. Detector BA is pooled across all 10 LLMs.
- MSumBench's self-bias quote and judge-by-generator table were not re-checked this pass.

## Q3. Papers the first pass missed (2025–2026, current-LLM outputs or fixed metric across eras)

### Takeaway
Two kinds of paper turned up:
- **Fixed metric across generator eras:** Zhong & Litman (Findings ACL 2025) is a direct comparison of evaluators on older summaries and on GPT-4-turbo-era summaries.
- **Benchmarks with fresh, frontier generators:** OmniCSEval (GPT-5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Qwen3; 2026), LongNovel (GPT-4o, Qwen3-32B; 2026) and FHSumBench (Findings ACL 2025).

The opposite pattern, a fresh judge on stale data, also continues into 2026. Atasoy et al. use GPT-5-mini and Gemini 2.5 Flash as judges on QAGS-C and SummEval, which are 2017–2020 outputs.

Excluded:
- Kulkarni et al., "The Mirage of Hallucination Detection" (Findings EMNLP 2025), tests metrics across 37 LMs from 5 families and finds they "do not show a clear monotonic improvement with an increase in model size". Its datasets are FaithDial, BEGIN and HaluEval QA/dialog, which are **not summarization**, so it is out of scope. It is still a fixed-metric-across-generator-sizes study in a nearby task ([ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1035.pdf)).
- A clinical-trial summary study (arXiv 2607.09932) scores GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Gemini 2.5 Flash summaries with an NLI model. I saw only a search snippet, with no human labels confirmed, so it is not given a row (UNCONFIRMED) ([arXiv 2607.09932](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09932v1)).

### Inferences
- The only 2025–2026 English summarization papers found with human-labelled GPT-5 or Gemini-2.5-era summaries are:
  - VeriGray: GPT-5, DeepSeek-V3, Qwen3-8B.
  - OmniCSEval: 28 LLMs, but only 120 human-labelled summaries, and it is a conversation-summarization benchmark.
- **No paper found evaluates human-labelled summaries from Claude 4.x, Gemini 2.x or Llama 3.3/4.**
- Zhong & Litman 2025 is the cleanest recent support, after Tang 2023 and UniSumEval, for the claim that evaluator accuracy measured on older summaries does not carry over to LLM summaries.
- The "fresh judge, stale data" pattern persists into 2026 (Atasoy et al.: GPT-5-mini judging 2017–2020 outputs, gap 6). It supports the first pass's Pattern 1.

### Gaps
- Several 2026 arXiv papers (OmniCSEval, LongNovel, Averaging Bias, Atasoy et al.) have no confirmed venue. Their main results were only partly read.
- I could not open the full FHSumBench generator list or SummExecEdit seed sources. The AlignScore 57.4% and MiniCheck 60.0% numbers for SummExecEdit come from a search snippet and were not checked in the PDF.
- Zhong & Litman's per-era recall and precision numbers were not extracted; only the abstract claim is verified.
- No systematic search of the EMNLP 2025 and ACL/NAACL 2026 proceedings was done. Other relevant papers may exist.
